# v2.1.0-beta.1 — Deck Plans & Destinations

Beta.1 completes two major public-site experiences. The new deck explorer supports every ship, seven guest decks, searchable cabins and venues, accessibility information, and responsive top-down diagrams.

The destinations area now includes eight regional collections with sailing seasons, representative voyage lengths, signature ports, shore-experience highlights, and dedicated detail pages connected to booking.

This is a demonstration release. Cabin maps are representational and booking does not store reservations or process payment.
